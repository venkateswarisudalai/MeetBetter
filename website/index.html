Features

Everything you need from a meeting assistant

Real-time transcription

Words appear as they're spoken. Powered by Deepgram's nova-2 with sub-second latency. Speaker labels distinguish who said what.

AI-powered summaries

Key points, action items, and decisions extracted automatically after every meeting. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Smart reply suggestions

Get contextual reply suggestions during live meetings. AI understands the conversation and helps you respond thoughtfully.

Calendar integration

Connect Google Calendar with one click. Auto-start transcription when meetings begin. No complex setup needed.

Privacy first

All processing stays on your device. Audio never leaves your machine. Hidden during screen sharing. No cloud, no data collection.

Cloud sync

Optionally back up meetings to the cloud. Toggle on in settings and your meetings auto-sync across sessions.

Setup

Get started in under a minute

Two free API keys. That's it.

1

Download & install

Download the .dmg, drag to Applications. On first launch, right-click and select "Open".

2

Get two free API keys

Deepgram Real-time transcription Includes $200 free credit Groq AI summaries & suggestions Free tier
3

Paste keys & go

Open Settings in Vantage, paste both keys, and you're ready. Join any meeting and hit "Start Meeting".
